# 02 · AREAS

**Ongoing responsibilities and the owner's intent.** Two things live here:

1. **Responsibilities** — the things you maintain indefinitely (a blog, a newsletter, a
   career track, a business, a consulting practice). Unlike a project, an area never
   "finishes" — it's a standing commitment.
2. **Opinion & structured intent** — *how you want things done*: voice guides ("write like
   me"), taste, strategy calls.

## The key distinction

- **Areas = your intent**, authoritative *to you*. "This is how I write." "This is what I'm
  building toward."
- **Resources (`04`)** = other people's material.
- **Permanent (`03`)** = distilled facts.

Keeping these apart means it's always obvious whether something is *your* stance or someone
else's — your opinions can't quietly blend into the factual base, and a borrowed voice guide
in `04` is never mistaken for your own.

## Notes

- An **area** note carries its **goals** explicitly and ends at `State`. It does **not** hand-maintain
  `Next` / `Open decisions` — the agent derives those on demand, so they can't go stale in the page.
- Opinion is *decaying* material: date it, attribute it, review it (~6 months).
- High-stakes guidance — voice/taste rules that shape every task — may be gated under
  `03 - PERMANENT/03.02 - guidance/` (not indexed, never auto-injected).
- This category grows with use: a new recurring responsibility becomes a new `02.MM` item.
