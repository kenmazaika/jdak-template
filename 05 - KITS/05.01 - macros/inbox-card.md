# Macro — write an Inbox message

**Trigger:** any moment an agent is about to put something in the owner's Inbox.

Every Inbox item is one communication with two reading lengths:

- **Title** — the subject or decision.
- **Summary** — the short version, understandable on its own.
- **Message** — the complete explanation, also understandable on its own.
- **Actions** — the choices.

## Workflow

1. Understand the issue and the source material.
2. **Write the complete message first**, as something you'd actually appreciate reading.
3. Read it as the owner would: someone who has not seen the research. Is anything confusing, excessive,
   awkward, missing, or hard to act on?
4. Revise the message.
5. Derive the summary from the finished message — the central point without losing essential context.
6. Check title, summary, message, and actions agree.

The message is the primary writing. Never leave it as raw research notes, internal shorthand, or a rough
handoff while polishing the summary.

## Standard

Both versions should sound like a thoughtful colleague: explain unfamiliar things when relevant, give
the context needed, say what is being proposed, and what approval would do. Keep evidence, uncertainty,
reasoning, and recommendations. Remove shorthand and self-reference. Be direct and economical.

No required sections, bullets, lengths, or templates. Sometimes two paragraphs; sometimes more. The
whole communication should be good writing.
